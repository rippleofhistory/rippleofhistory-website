A kit bag, not a palace

The films stay free. The morning short, the long ones, Sunday on the seafront. That is the arrangement, and it is staying.

What a coffee pays for is ordinary. Petrol out to a tower. A mic that copes with the wind off the Channel. The hour spent finishing the edit. Nothing here is building a palace.

If you would like to put something in, a proper coffee is the one I hope for. Kit, if the mood takes you. A day out, if the next fortress is going to happen at all.

  • £3 Coffee
  • £10 Kit
  • £25 Day out

They open the same jar. Choose the amount when you arrive, or ignore the suggestions entirely.

If you would rather not, watch the next one and pass it on. That still counts. Thank you for reading this far.

Daniel
