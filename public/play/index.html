HOW TO PLAY

MOVE
A / D or Arrow Keys
JUMP
W, Up, or D-pad ▲
ROLL
S, Down, or D-pad ▼ — dash, duck fire
LIGHT
J or Z — close blade / bayonet / burst. 3-hit combo
HEAVY
K or X — long spear, bash, cleave, or a gun / drone
BLOCK
L or C — hold, no movement
MOUNT
Space — horse, cart, jeep, or tank
MUTE
M — silence the tether
PAUSE
Esc
PAD
Stick / D-pad · A jump · X light · Y special · B block

Each era locks its own weapon — light is close steel, heavy is the era’s special. Pits kill; jump the layered stones, and don’t linger on cracking planks. Vehicles return only at checkpoints. Beat a boss without taking a hit on that life for a bonus tether. Lives and cleared eras persist.
