What to send

  • A correction — the date, the video or calendar day, and the source if you have one. I would rather be right than stubborn.
  • Press or a booking — who you are, the deadline, and what you need. Eastbourne and the South East are easiest in person.
  • A rights question — say whether it is about a film, a still, or the wordmark.

Please do not send attachments you would not want sitting in an inbox. Do not send payment card details by email.
